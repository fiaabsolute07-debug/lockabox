FAQ

Questions ser?

Is rolling really free?

Yes. Rolling never asks for a signature or a payment. Only buying does, and you sign that in your own wallet.

Do you hold my money?

No. Lockabox never holds funds or keys. Swaps go through Jupiter or a DEX router, straight from your wallet.

How do I know the roll isn't rigged?

The seed hash is published before you roll. After the seed rotates, open Verify and recompute any pull yourself.

Is this financial advice?

No. It's a random pick. Memecoins can go to zero. Only buy what you can afford to lose.
