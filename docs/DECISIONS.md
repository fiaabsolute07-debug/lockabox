KyberSwap has no key and covers all five chains but returns "access from your country is restricted" from Vietnam, so calling it from a server elsewhere would sidestep its terms; 0x/1inch need an account. LI.FI works with no key on all five chains, but: (1) LI.FI takes its own fixed **0.25 %** on every route (shown in the quote as a route fee; Lockabox's fee stays 0); (2) its terms exclude **US persons** and ask integrators to say the integration is independent and non-custodial. Sell check: honeypot.is simulation on Ethereum/BSC/Base, LI.FI buy→sell quote round trip on Robinhood/Arc (weaker, like #5). Guards on every quote: right chain, sender, token, amount, value, LI.FI contract as target, no integrator fee, fee-collection ≤ 0.25 %. Arc buys pay in USDC and need an exact-amount approve first. |
